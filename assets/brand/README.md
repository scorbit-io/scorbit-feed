required clear space is already included in the artwork. Do not crop, recolour,

## Usage notes

From Scorbit's identity guidelines:

- **Clear space:** keep a margin of one quarter of a letter's height in the
  wordmark free of other elements. The files already include it.
- **Minimum size (digital, including clear space):** horizontal lockup 30 px
  tall, symbol 38 px, vertical lockup 50 px.
- **Backgrounds:** use the logo on solid backgrounds with ample contrast. On a
  photographic or video background, keep the full-colour logo on its black or
  white strap (these files have it built in), or use an all-white version.
- Prefer the horizontal lockup in wide layouts and the vertical lockup in square
  or tall ones.
